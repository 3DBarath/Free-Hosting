Deployment Guide:

  • Rename your main(entry) html as index.html‼️
  • Place your html,css,js files in a folder.
  • Compress that folder and Rename it(This name will be your Project Name‼️)
  • The Project Name should not contains space or special characters (ie. "MY Project"❌,"MYProject$"❌)
  • Click the upload area or drag & drop your ZIP file
  • Smash the Deploy Button.💥
  • If another Project with same name exist,you will be prompted,Then enter a different Project Name
  • Wait for the upload to complete.
  • Access your live preview link immediately.🚀
  • Share your project with your friends and family!✨
